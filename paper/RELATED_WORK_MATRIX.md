# AiWingman related-work and claim-boundary matrix

Status: literature audit for the major-revision manuscript, updated 24 August 2026.
This is a targeted primary-source audit, not a systematic review. It does not
support a claim that AiWingman is first or unique.

## Defensible positioning after the audit

Existing research and products already provide developer dashboards, task
contexts, resumption cues, parallel agent sessions, history summaries, usage
inspection, and agent-assisted critique. AiWingman therefore makes no first or
superiority claim for those capabilities.

The defensible contribution is an implementation-specific design case that
combines locally retained Codex task evidence, offline-default analysis,
reversible user-owned lifecycle labels, and deterministic ranking suppression
under explicit evidence rules. Direct SQLite and deep-link access are
unsupported compatibility choices, not novelty. The ordinary
dashboard reader and optional Wingman task-tree reader are separate pipelines
and must be reported separately. The design case is not evidence of improved
resumption, productivity, decision quality, or token efficiency.
